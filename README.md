A Heartfelt Message to the People of the World
Dear brothers and sisters around the world,
Today I speak not as a politician, not as a government official, but as a human being — as a son or daughter of Iran — with a heavy heart and tears in my eyes.
Our country is bleeding. Our people are suffering. Every day this war continues, more innocent lives are lost, more families are destroyed, more children lose their future, and more pain is added to an already wounded nation. The bombs do not distinguish between soldier and civilian. The suffering does not stop at the border.
We, the people of Iran, are tired of war. We do not want death. We do not want destruction. We want to live. We want to breathe freely. We want our mothers to stop crying, our children to go to school without fear, and our youth to dream again instead of running from explosions.
To the people of the world, I beg you:
Please do not stay silent.
Please do not look away.
Please raise your voice for Iran.
Help us end this war quickly.
Help us push for an immediate ceasefire.
Help us demand serious negotiations and a fair, permanent peace deal.
Your voice matters. When millions of people around the world stand together and say “Stop the War on Iran”, governments are forced to listen. When you share, when you protest peacefully, when you sign petitions, when you speak to your politicians — you become the light of hope for the Iranian people.
We are not asking you to choose sides in politics.
We are asking you to choose humanity.
Choose the side of mothers who lost their children.
Choose the side of fathers who can no longer feed their families.
Choose the side of peace over destruction.
To the kind and compassionate people of America, Europe, Russia, China, Turkey, India, Pakistan, and every corner of this Earth:
Your solidarity can save lives. Your silence hurts.
Let us unite as human beings — beyond flags, beyond politics, beyond hatred. Let the world hear one clear message:
“End the War on Iran Now!”
“Let Iran Live in Peace!”
“We Demand Ceasefire and Permanent Peace Today!”
The time is now. Every single day counts. Every voice counts. Every share counts.
Please stand with the people of Iran in this dark hour. History will remember those who chose compassion when it was needed most.
From the bottom of my heart, on behalf of millions of Iranians who only want peace:
Thank you.
We will never forget your kindness.
#EndWarOnIran
#CeasefireNow
#PeaceForIran
